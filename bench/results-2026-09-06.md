# Hierarchical plotting benchmark — 2026-09-06

Local machine and bundled DGGRID 9.0b. Timings are elapsed wall-clock time and
are intended as development evidence, not performance guarantees.

The source pool contained 100,000 distinct global IGEO7 resolution-12 cells.
Larger inputs repeated that pool so the benchmark could isolate scaling of
index mapping and value aggregation without first generating ten million
coordinates.

| Source rows | Ancestors at res 4 | Aggregate at res 4 | LOD selection to res 2 | Full LOD preparation |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 100,000 | 0.012 s | 0.031 s | 0.687 s | 1.012 s |
| 1,000,000 | 0.101 s | 0.267 s | 0.993 s | 1.378 s |
| 10,000,000 | 1.001 s | 2.017 s | 4.148 s | 7.748 s |

The resolution-4 aggregation produced 23,616 parent groups. Full global LOD
preparation selected 492 legitimate resolution-2 cells and includes viewport
selection, aggregation, final polygon generation, and conversion to `sf`.

## Naive versus hierarchical polygon display at 100,000 cells

| Stage | Naive source polygons | Hierarchical LOD polygons |
|---|---:|---:|
| Geometry creation / full preparation | 7.459 s for 100,000 polygons | 0.974 s for 492 polygons |
| Base graphics rendering | 2.671 s | 0.076 s |

This is roughly an 8x reduction in preparation time and a 35x reduction in
rendering time for this probe. More importantly, the 10-million-row LOD path
still sent only 492 polygons to the graphics device.

## Polygon versus raster display

The automatic renderer was measured on a 1200 by 650 pixel PNG. Geometry was
prepared once for the 5,000- and 20,000-cell probes so that device work could
be compared directly.

| Display cells | Geometry preparation | Vector device | Rasterize + device | Vector PNG | Raster PNG |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5,000 | 1.868 s | 0.142 s | 2.856 s | 177 kB | 69 kB |
| 20,000 | 4.053 s | 4.971 s | 1.187 s | 380 kB | 143 kB |

On the complete phosphorus example with 46,845 native resolution-5 cells,
end-to-end polygon plotting took 55.985 seconds and raster plotting took
32.416 seconds. The corresponding PNGs were 311 kB and 148 kB. This supports
the default automatic crossover at 20,000 displayed cells while leaving the
threshold user-configurable.
